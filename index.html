Compatibility Notice

Before you step in

This game is likely not compatible for Lower devices to handle High graphics, do you want to turn on Potato Mode? You can Turn it on at the Settings on the right top of your screen or Press ESC if you're on PC.

Potato Mode disables dense grass, mist and shadows. The trees stay.

Your choice only affects this session. You can change it any time in Settings.
